FROM REPOSITORY TO BATTLEFIELD

Your server. Step by step.

This GitHub page introduces the game. Render runs the full game and its multiplayer server.

  1. Sign in to Render

    Open Render’s dashboard and sign in with GitHub. Allow Render access to DeepInkGroup/deep-front.

  2. Create a Blueprint

    Choose New → Blueprint. Connect DeepInkGroup/deep-front, select branch main, name it deep-front, and keep the Blueprint path render.yaml.

  3. Review and deploy

    Confirm one Node web service, the Free plan, and Frankfurt. The repository supplies the build command, start command, environment variables, and health check. Click Deploy Blueprint.

  4. Wait for Live

    Open the service to follow its build logs. Once the status is Live, copy the HTTPS onrender.com URL shown on its page. Render chooses the final hostname.

  5. Invite your friends

    Open that URL, choose Host a game, and share the lobby invite. Your friends join the same server. Start the match once everyone is ready.

ⓘ

The free service sleeps after 15 minutes without traffic and can take about a minute to wake. Restarting or deploying ends active matches. Creator accounts and published maps require persistent storage. Account and map storage setup ↗ Free hosting details ↗
